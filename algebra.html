Algebra

Learn algebraic expressions, equations, inequalities, factorization and other fundamental algebraic concepts.

What is Algebra?

Algebra is a branch of mathematics that uses letters, numbers and symbols to represent quantities and relationships between quantities.

Example:

2x + 5 = 15

Here, x represents an unknown value.

Algebraic Expressions

An algebraic expression consists of numbers, variables and mathematical operations.

Examples

  • 3x + 5
  • 2x² + 4x - 7
  • 5a - 3b
  • x² + 9

Linear Equations

A linear equation is an equation in which the highest power of the variable is 1.

Example

Solve: 

2x + 6 = 14

2x = 14 - 6

2x = 8

x = 4

Factorization

Factorization is the process of writing an expression as a product of its factors.

Example

x² + 5x + 6

= (x + 2)(x + 3)

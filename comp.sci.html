About This Course

Computer Science is the study of computation, information, algorithms and computer systems. This course introduces you to the fundamental ideas needed to understand how computers work and how software is developed.

You will develop problem-solving skills and learn how to approach real-world problems using computational thinking.

Learning Modules

Follow the modules in order to build your knowledge step by step.

01

Introduction to Computer Science

Understand computer science, its history, applications and importance.

02

Computer Systems

Learn about hardware, software, memory, processors and computer components.

03

Algorithms & Problem Solving

Learn how algorithms are designed and used to solve computational problems.

04

Programming Fundamentals

Learn variables, data types, operators, conditions, loops and functions.

05

Data Structures

Explore arrays, stacks, queues, linked lists and other ways of organizing data.

06

Computer Ethics

Understand privacy, security, intellectual property and ethical computing.

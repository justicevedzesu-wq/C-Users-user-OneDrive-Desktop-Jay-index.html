Computer Ethics

Understand the ethical responsibilities involved in the use and development of computer technology.

What is Computer Ethics?

Computer ethics refers to the principles and standards that guide responsible behaviour when using computers and information technology.

Major Ethical Issues

  • Privacy
  • Security
  • Accuracy
  • Intellectual property
  • Accessibility
  • Cybercrime

Privacy

Privacy involves protecting people's personal information from unauthorized collection, use or disclosure.

Security

Computer security involves protecting computer systems, networks and information against unauthorized access, damage or attacks.

Intellectual Property

Intellectual property refers to creations of the human mind, such as software, inventions, written works and designs.

Using someone else's work without proper permission or attribution may violate intellectual property rights.

Key Principle

Use technology responsibly, respect people's rights, protect information and avoid causing harm.
